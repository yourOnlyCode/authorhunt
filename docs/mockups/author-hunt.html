The Salt Road Almanac

A mapmaker's daughter inherits an atlas that redraws itself each winter.

MO
by Maren Okafor-Lindqvist  
Get the bookSupport the author
AboutExcerptComments 42The authorMore by Maren

Every winter the atlas in Odile's attic changes. Roads move. A town she has visited is gone from the page, and then gone from the valley. Her mother drew it, and her mother's debts came with it.

The Salt Road Almanac is a novel about inheritance and weather, and about what a map owes the people it leads astray.

Content notesDeath of a parent, drowning, and a house fire. No graphic violence.Shown only if you ask.
