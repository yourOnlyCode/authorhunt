MO

Maren Okafor-Lindqvist

Fantasy about maps, weather and inheritance. Raleigh, North Carolina.

1,204 readers follow

I trained as a cartographer and spent eight years drawing flood maps for the state before I wrote a word of fiction. My books are about people who are handed a map and told it's finished.

The Salt Road Almanac is my second novel. I'm writing the third now, a chapter a week, and posting what I learn here.
